Solmar City is an original open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser from static files — no build step, no network requests. You are Leo Marlow, a driver working for the fixer Ms. Vega in a sun-soaked fictional coastal city. Explore Solmar on foot or behind the wheel of a stolen car, weave through traffic that now steers around you (and piles up when it can't), and try not to catch the eye of the Solmar police: every crime raises your wanted level from 0 to 5 stars, patrol cars will chase you down and ram you off the road, and on foot a patrol car that stops beside you fills a bust meter — run, or you're going downtown in cuffs. Work through seven jobs from the in-game phone: five story missions (steal and deliver a car, run a timed package route, lose a 3-star heat, collect a debt the hard way, pull off the Marina Heist) plus two repeatable side jobs (gather crates on the beach for the bonfire crew, shuffle cargo at the port), while the HUD tracks your cash, health, ammo, wanted stars, the radio track and a live minimap. A hurt car handles worse and its engine sputters; a near miss at speed pays cash; a shot-out tyre leaves a traffic car limping. All art is drawn in canvas and all sound — including the three-track synth radio — is synthesised with WebAudio, so the whole game is original and self-contained.

## Run it
```sh
node serve.ts
```
(then open http://localhost:8080)

## Controls
- **WASD / arrows** — move on foot, drive in a car · **Shift** — run
- **E** — steal / enter a car, step out · **Space** — handbrake
- **Mouse aim, click** — shoot the pistol · **F** — punch · **H** — horn (near the police, a crime)
- **M** — phone (mission list, press 1–7 to start a job) · **Esc / P** — pause
- **N** — mute · **R** — get back up after being busted or wasted
